**LFS SRE Tools 2.0.5** is out. Blender offers the update in Get Extensions (or at the next start, if *Check for Updates on Startup* is ticked).

**Fixes:**
- **Blender closing during Bake.** With the model's own textures, when the bake had to start again with fewer maps, Blender could crash. Fixed.
- **Export no longer changes a part's position.** The body, interior and wheels go out at 0, 0, 0, so their centre line is the car's and the editor's mirror lines up. The steering wheel keeps its pivot, because LFS turns it around that point.
- **Auto Setup puts the parts at the top of the Outliner.** On a 3ds Max car they were folded away under its empties. The empties left over go into a switched-off collection, "Auto Setup leftovers".

**Auto Setup (3ds Max cars):**
- **Half models:** some cars come over with only one half of the outside, because Max mirrored it and the mirror didn't survive the conversion. That left one side of the car open. LFS now mirrors that half, with its centre line exactly on the car's middle.
- **Empty copies:** a right door or a wheel that came over as an empty object with nothing in it now gets the pieces it copies.

**Bake:**
- **Whole pieces instead of scraps:** each object is painted from as few views as it needs. A lamp, badge, screen or dial is one picture; a seat is two or three. Inside the car, pieces are sized by their detail and by how well the driver sees them, so the gauges and the climate panel are big and the floor mats are small.
- **Each part has its own list of maps** (236 each). Before, the whole car shared one list. That means far more pieces and sharper pages: the Omega's interior went from 89 to 139 pixels per metre.
- **Fuller pages:** pieces grow into the room left on their page. Plain plastic gets fewer pixels; dials, vents and buttons get more.
- **The steering wheel is one picture seen from the driver.** Its back and sides no longer take room on its page.
- Tiled seat fabric and carpet get a map for each direction they face again, so they don't stretch over curves.

**New:** LFS tab > **LFS Texture for Selected Faces** (Edit Mode). Select faces (seats, a door card...) and give them LFS leather, LFS carpet, a plain colour, Bake or Bake sharper. Then run Bake again.
