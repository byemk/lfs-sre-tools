**LFS SRE Tools 2.0.4** is out - Blender offers the update in Get Extensions (or at the next start, if *Check for Updates on Startup* is ticked).

**Export:**
- **Far fewer smoothing groups:** a part made in Blender used all 24 of LFS's groups (the editor showed 23 or 24) - now only as many as its edges need (an Audi RSQ3: 7 / 6 / 5 / 4), so there are groups left for what you model in the editor. The hard edges are exactly where they were, and two pieces that only touch at a corner no longer blend there.
- **Reflection colours stay white** - the part's colour is in its map. Only window glass keeps its dark reflection, and a see-through page that is not glass (a grille, a decal) reflects like plastic, not like glass.

**Auto Setup (models from other formats):**
- **A car 10 times too big** (a 3ds Max model in decimetres, 58 m long) is scaled to metres by its wheels - it came out as one part, the rest left out as "scenery".
- **Part names in more languages** - Turkish, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Polish, Dutch (direksiyon, jant, lastik, koltuk, Lenkrad, Felge, Sitz, asiento...) - and a mesh whose name says nothing takes its parent's (a seat under an empty called "interior").
- A car's underside (black, under, bottom, chassis...) is no longer taken for glass.

**Bake:**
- **No more "Map limit (255) reached"** on cars with lots of textured materials: when the model's own textures would need more maps than LFS allows, the bake keeps fewer of them, or paints everything into new pages, and finishes.
- **The back of the steering wheel** (nobody sees it from the seat) gets a quarter of the pixels, so the front gets the room - about 18 % sharper.

**Please try:** a downloaded car (Auto Setup -> Bake Textures for LFS -> Export), especially one that came out with missing parts or stopped with an error before. Tell me what looks wrong (LFS tab -> **Copy Bug Report**, plus a screenshot).
